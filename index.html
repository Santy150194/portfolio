01
60–70% Crack Rejection Crisis — Contained & Resolved
GM Program · High-Volume CNC Line
Incoming castings had 60–70% crack rejection rate. Used SQL to segment CMM data by batch and supplier, identified root cause as improper heat treatment at casting source. Issued 8D, quarantined all suspect material via RDAC, defined 100% incoming CMM inspection protocol. Zero customer escapes. Supplier corrective action implemented and sustained.

02
Seal Mix-Up Poka-Yoke — Zero Recurrence
Tesla Semi · Leak Test Line
Wrong seals being loaded into leak test fixtures causing false failures and scrapped assemblies. Traced root cause to identical-looking seal SKUs stored adjacent on the line. Implemented color-coded seal storage, physical poka-yoke on fixture loading, and barcode scan verification before cycle start. Zero recurrence. Plant-wide read-across completed to all similar lines.
8DPoka-YokeBarcode VerificationRDAC Serial TrackingVisual Standard Work

In-House Assembly COPQ Business Case — Approved
Tesla Semi · Metal Technologies Inc.
Assembly was being outsourced causing transportation damage, longer lead times, and loss of process control. Built full COPQ analysis using SQL production data and Grafana dashboards showing $X weekly cost of external assembly plus damage rate. Presented ROI model to management. In-house assembly approved. Transportation damage eliminated. OEE and traceability improved from day one.

3 of 5 CNCs Down Simultaneously — No Quality Escapes
Production Crisis · 40% Capacity · Customer Delivery at Risk
Three of five CNC machines failed simultaneously — spindle runout, fixture failure, mechanical breakdown. Capacity dropped to 40%. Immediately quarantined all suspect parts from down machines and verified serial traceability in RDAC. Ran rapid process verification on running machines — tool check, CMM verification, SPC active. Triaged recovery: quick win first, then fixture repair, then external maintenance. Adjusted production schedule in real time using Grafana OEE dashboards. All three machines recovered in priority order. Zero quality escapes. Customer delivery held without exception.

05
GD&T Print Review — 5–6 Ambiguities Caught Pre-Launch
Tesla NPI · Design Collaboration
During NPI print review for a new Tesla program, identified 5–6 features with missing or ambiguous GD&T callouts — including a critical bore with diameter tolerance but no position callout to datum reference frame, making CMM inspection impossible. Documented each issue with the specific machining challenge and inspection gap it created. Presented to Tesla design engineers framed as risk to their launch timeline. All features updated before production release. Part launched clean and on schedule. Tesla design team now requests manufacturability reviews proactively before finalizing drawings.

07
Thermal Cell Design — Temperature-Controlled Assembly Zone
Tesla Semi · Process Cell Redesign
After diagnosing thermal distortion as root cause of Cpk failures, the new process required the part to cool to 68–70°F ±2°C before final machining. Designed a dedicated temperature-controlled cooling soak area adjacent to assembly — eliminating the transport loop, controlling ambient temperature, and adding a go/no-go temperature indicator before parts could advance to CNC. Datum stability achieved. Post-assembly variation eliminated entirely as a failure category. Cpk improved from 1.33 to above 1.67 after cell implementation.
Cell DesignThermal ControlProcess EngineeringLean LayoutError-Proofing
08
FA Gauging System — Eliminated Manual Inspection Bottleneck
Tesla Semi · GM L6 · OEE Improvement
Manual CMM inspection after each operation was creating a bottleneck — parts queuing for inspection, operators idle, OEE taking a performance hit. Designed and implemented in-line functional audit (FA) gauging with go/no-go fixtures at critical operations. Reduced inspection time from 12 minutes per part to under 90 seconds. 5 hours per week of unplanned downtime eliminated. Throughput improved 30%. Parts flow continuously without inspection queues.
